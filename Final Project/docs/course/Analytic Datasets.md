# Analytic Datasets

## Overview

The course datasets are based on the **National Inpatient Sample (NIS)** and are organized into multiple analytic projects. Each project corresponds to a different clinical or health services research question.

## Project Dataset List

Click the appropriate course link to access the dataset assigned to your project.

| Project | Research Question |
| --- | --- |
| **Project 1** | Compare the outcomes of **open vs. laparoscopic colectomy** in patients with **colorectal cancer** |
| **Project 2** | Does **hospital volume** influence outcomes in patients hospitalized for **pneumonia**? |
| **Project 3** | Does treatment at a **teaching hospital** influence outcomes among patients hospitalized with **pyelonephritis**? |
| **Project 4** | Compare **mortality outcomes** of **gastrectomy** among patients with **gastric ulcer with perforation** |
| **Project 5** | Do outcomes differ for patients undergoing **open versus thoracoscopic lobectomy** for **lung cancer**? |
| **Project 6** | Compare the outcomes of **laparoscopic vs. open hysterectomy** in patients undergoing the procedure |
| **Project 7** | Compare the occurrence of **stroke and mortality** among postpartum women who received services at **black-serving hospitals** versus **non-black-serving hospitals** |
| **Project 8** | Compare the outcomes of **laparoscopic vs. open oophorectomy** in patients with **benign ovarian disease** |
